Meet the Team

The Coaches Behind Peak Mobility Yoga

Two instructors, one shared belief: yoga should be approachable, affordable, and welcoming — no matter where you're starting from.

Kelsey Lovett

Founder & Lead Instructor

Peak Mobility Yoga was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves access to movement, breath, and community — regardless of budget or experience level. After more than six years of teaching yoga throughout Aiken, Kelsey opened a dedicated studio space inside Total Vibe Salon and Studios, built around the same values that have guided her practice from the start.

Every class is designed to be approachable for beginners while still offering depth for experienced yogis. No judgment, no intimidation — just a supportive room, a mat, and a little space to move at your own pace.

  • 6+ years teaching yoga in the Aiken community
  • 10-member open floor plan studio for a personal, low-pressure class size

Taylor Wood

Coach

Taylor is a 200-hour RYT who deepened her practice to find presence, calm, and mental clarity amid a busy life. Off the mat, she's a mom of three, dog mom of three, and a corporate executive leading national communications.

She brings a deep focus on grounding and authentic connection to every class, helping you find space to reset and recharge — no matter how full your week looks.

Catch her new Tuesday evening Mobility Flow class, starting November 24th.
