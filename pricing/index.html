Which option is right for me?

  • Trying it out — Drop-In

    If you're not sure yoga is for you yet, or you just want to try a single class, the $18 drop-in has no commitment at all.

  • 1–2x a week — 10-Class Pack

    At $14 per class and valid for 12 months, the pack is the better deal once you're coming regularly but not every day.

  • 3+ times a week — Monthly Membership

    At $99/month, unlimited pays for itself once you're attending more than about 5–6 classes a month ($99 ÷ $18 ≈ 5.5 classes) — no contract, cancel anytime.
