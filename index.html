Our Story

Six years of teaching Aiken to breathe a little easier.

Peak Mobility Yoga was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves access to movement, breath, and community — regardless of budget or experience level. After more than six years of teaching yoga throughout Aiken, our founder opened a dedicated studio space inside Total Vibe Salon and Studios, built around the same values that have guided her practice from the start.

Every class is designed to be approachable for beginners while still offering depth for experienced yogis. No judgment, no intimidation — just a supportive room, a mat, and a little space to move at your own pace. Classes stay small on purpose, so your instructor can actually see how you're moving and offer hands-on cues instead of talking to a crowd.

  • 6+ years teaching yoga in the Aiken community
  • 10-member open floor plan studio for a personal, low-pressure class size
  • Affordable pricing with drop-in and multi-class options

New Here?

Never done yoga before? Perfect.

Every class is beginner-friendly, mats and props are provided, and you'll know exactly what to expect before you ever step on the mat. Just bring water, wear something comfortable, and plan to arrive about 10 minutes early so you have time to sign in and settle in.
