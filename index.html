Our Story

Six years of teaching Aiken to breathe a little easier.

Peak Mobility Yoga was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves access to movement, breath, and community — regardless of budget or experience level. After more than six years of teaching yoga throughout Aiken, our founder is opening a dedicated studio space inside Total Vibe Salon, built around the same values that have guided her practice from the start.

Every class is designed to be approachable for beginners while still offering depth for experienced yogis. No judgment, no intimidation — just a supportive room, a mat, and a little space to move at your own pace.

  • 6+ years teaching yoga in the Aiken community
  • 15-member open floor plan studio for a personal, low-pressure class size
  • Affordable pricing with drop-in and multi-class options
Our Mission

Making fitness & mindful movement affordable and welcoming for everyone in Aiken.

What We Offer

Find your flow

Every class fits inside our open floor plan studio, capped at 15 members so your instructor can actually see you.

Slow Flow

A relaxed, therapeutic pace built around breath and release — geared toward a calm, unhurried mindset.

Functional Fitness Flow

Movement-based sequences that build real-world strength, mobility, and balance — a focus woven into most of our classes.

Vinyasa & Advanced Flow

Breath-linked, dynamic sequences for practitioners ready to build heat and deepen their practice.

Performance & Recovery

Deep stretching and conditioning to improve performance and recovery.
