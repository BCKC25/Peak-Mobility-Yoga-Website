FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience?

No. Beginners are welcome in every class, and Beginner Yoga is designed specifically for people brand-new to the practice.

What should I bring?

Just water and comfortable clothes.

Do you provide mats?

Yes, mats and props are provided.

How many people are in a class?

Classes are capped at 10 people.

How early should I arrive?

About 10 minutes early, to sign in.

Where do I park?

Free parking is available in the shared lot out front.

Where is the studio?

1510 Whiskey Rd, Aiken, SC 29803, inside Total Vibe Salon and Studios.

How much is a drop-in?

$18 per class.

How long is a 10-pack valid?

12 months from the date of purchase.

Is there a contract on the membership?

No. The $99/month Unlimited Membership has no contract.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, cancel anytime.

Which class should I take first?

Beginner Yoga, if you're new to the practice.

Do I book online?

Yes, you can book a class or buy a pass online.

What's the FALL50 promo?

New members who sign up with a friend get their first month of Unlimited for $50.

Do you offer private sessions?

Yes, one-on-one and small-group sessions are available by appointment.

Who are the instructors?

Kelsey Lovett, founder and lead instructor with 6+ years teaching yoga in Aiken, and Taylor Wood, a 200-hour RYT.
