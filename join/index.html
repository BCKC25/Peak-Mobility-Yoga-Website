What to expect at Peak Mobility Yoga

Peak Mobility Yoga is a welcoming, affordable yoga studio at 1510 Whiskey Rd in Aiken, SC 29803. Every class is approachable for beginners while still offering depth for experienced yogis, and classes are capped at 10 members so your instructor can actually see you.

  • 14 classes a week, all 7 days, from early morning to evening
  • Beginner Yoga, Vinyasa Flow, Mobility Flow, and Gentle Yoga classes
  • Simple pricing: $18 drop-in, $140 for a 10-class pack, or $99/month for unlimited classes with no contract
