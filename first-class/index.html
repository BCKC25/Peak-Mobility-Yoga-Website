Your First Class

Here's exactly what to expect before you ever step on the mat.

  • What to bring

    Mats and props are provided — just bring water and wear comfortable clothes.

  • When to arrive

    Plan to arrive about 10 minutes early to sign in and get settled before class starts.

  • Where to park

    Free parking is available in the shared lot right out front.

  • Finding the studio

    Peak Mobility Yoga is inside Total Vibe Salon and Studios at 1510 Whiskey Rd, Aiken, SC 29803. See directions and parking details.

  • Which class to start with

    If you're new to yoga or easing back into practice, start with Beginner Yoga. It moves at a slower pace and covers the fundamentals.

  • What if I need to modify a pose

    Every pose can be modified. Just let your instructor know at the start of class and they'll offer an easier variation — no judgment, ever.
