Beginner Yoga

Beginner Yoga in Aiken

A gentle, approachable introduction to the fundamentals — no experience necessary.

Book This Class

Who it's for

Beginner Yoga is for anyone who has never set foot on a mat, anyone returning to practice after time away, and anyone who wants to move slowly enough to actually learn the fundamentals. It's also a comfortable fit for students with stiffness, limited mobility, or any concern that a faster-paced class might move too quickly for them.

What a class feels like

Class opens with a short grounding warm-up — breath work and gentle joint mobility to ease into the body. From there, the instructor walks through foundational postures one at a time, explaining alignment and offering modifications as you go, rather than moving through a fast-flowing sequence. The pace leaves room for questions. Class closes with a longer cool-down and a few minutes of rest.

Intensity level

Low. This is the most approachable class on the schedule, designed so you leave feeling looser and calmer, not wiped out.

When it runs

Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 10:00 AM, plus Tuesday and Thursday at 8:00 AM. See the full weekly schedule.

Who teaches it

Beginner Yoga is led by Kelsey Lovett, founder and lead instructor at Peak Mobility Yoga.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience for Beginner Yoga?

No. This class is built for people who have never done yoga before, as well as anyone easing back into practice.

What should I bring?

Just water and comfortable clothes. Mats and props are provided.

How big is the class?

Capped at 10 people, so your instructor has time to help with your form.

When does Beginner Yoga meet?

Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 10:00 AM, and Tuesday and Thursday at 8:00 AM.

Who teaches Beginner Yoga?

Kelsey Lovett, founder and lead instructor at Peak Mobility Yoga.
