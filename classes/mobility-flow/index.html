Mobility Flow

Mobility Flow: Strength + Flexibility

Movement-based sequences that build real-world strength, mobility, and balance.

Book This Class

Who it's for

Mobility Flow is built for anyone who wants to move better in daily life or sport — desk workers with tight hips, weekend athletes managing nagging stiffness, and anyone who wants to work on joint range of motion and balance alongside a regular yoga practice.

What a class feels like

Class starts with a warm-up that mobilizes the major joints — hips, shoulders, spine — before moving into a sequence built around functional movement patterns: controlled lunges, deep stretches held with intention, and balance work. It's less about flowing quickly from pose to pose and more about building strength through a full range of motion. Class winds down with a longer, deeper stretch and a few minutes of stillness.

Intensity level

Moderate, with a focus on control and range of motion rather than speed.
