Vinyasa Flow

Vinyasa Flow Yoga

Breath-linked, dynamic sequences that build heat and strength, moving from one posture to the next.

Book This Class

Who it's for

Vinyasa Flow is built for students who have at least a little mat time under them and want to move continuously, link breath to movement, and build heat. It's a good next step once Beginner Yoga starts to feel comfortable, and a solid regular practice for anyone who already knows the basics.

What a class feels like

Class opens with a short warm-up to connect breath and movement, then builds into a flowing sequence of sun salutations and standing postures, each movement linked to an inhale or exhale. The pace keeps moving — less time holding any one posture, more time transitioning through them — building toward a peak sequence before a slower cool-down and a few minutes of stillness at the end.

Intensity level

Moderate to vigorous. Expect to build heat and work up a sweat.

When it runs

Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 8:00 AM. See the full weekly schedule.

Who teaches it

Vinyasa Flow is led by Kelsey Lovett, founder and lead instructor at Peak Mobility Yoga.

Frequently asked questions

Is Vinyasa Flow good for beginners?

It moves faster than our Beginner Yoga class, so we recommend having at least a little mat time first. If you're brand new, start with Beginner Yoga.

What should I bring?

Just water and comfortable clothes. Mats and props are provided.

How intense is this class?

Moderate to vigorous. Expect to build heat and work up a sweat.

When does Vinyasa Flow meet?

Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 8:00 AM.

Who teaches Vinyasa Flow?

Kelsey Lovett, founder and lead instructor at Peak Mobility Yoga.
